# EE-223 — Digital Logic Design

**University:** Sarhad University of Science & Information Technology, Peshawar  
**Department:** Computer Science and IT  
**Credit Hours:** 3-0  
**Course Duration:** One Semester  
**Credit Units:** 3  
**Level:** 3rd Semester, 2nd Year  
**Prerequisites:** None

## Course Objectives

The course introduces combinational and sequential circuit analysis and design, and digital circuit design optimization using logic gates, multiplexers, decoders, registers, and counters. It teaches the basic concepts of conventional digital design and how digital circuits are designed and analyzed.

## Course Learning Outcomes

### CLO-01
Identify the fundamental concepts of digital logic design including basic and universal gates, number systems, and basic components of combinational and sequential circuits.

### CLO-02
Apply techniques related to the design and analysis of digital electronic circuits including Boolean algebra and multi-variable K-map methods.

### CLO-03
Construct small-scale combinational and sequential logic circuits, including latches, flip-flops, registers, and counters.
