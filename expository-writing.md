# ENG316 — Expository Writing

**University:** Sarhad University of Science & Information Technology, Peshawar  
**Department:** Computer Science and IT  
**Credit Hours:** 3-0  
**Course Duration:** One Semester  
**Credit Units:** 2  
**Level:** Undergraduate  
**Prerequisites:** None

## Course Objectives

This course develops proficiency in different forms of writing, including paragraph and essay writing, descriptive and persuasive writing, presentations, reviews, short stories, dialogues, and letters. It aims to improve the organization and expression of ideas in written and oral formats.

Students will be able to:

- Assess writing situations and produce well-structured written content.
- Develop and organize ideas logically in different writing formats.
- Maintain a consistent viewpoint and use appropriate writing techniques for expository and practical writing.

## Course Learning Outcomes

### CLO-01
Demonstrate proficiency in various writing styles and their applications in different contexts.

### CLO-02
Effectively communicate ideas and arguments through clear, coherent, and well-organized writing.

### CLO-03
Engage in self-assessment and peer review to improve writing skills and adopt lifelong learning practices.
