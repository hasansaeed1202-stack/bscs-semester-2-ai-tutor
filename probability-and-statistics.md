# MA-313 — Probability and Statistics

**University:** Sarhad University of Science & Information Technology, Peshawar  
**Department:** Computer Science and IT  
**Credit Hours:** 3-0  
**Course Duration:** One Semester  
**Credit Units:** 3  
**Level:** 2nd Semester, 1st Year  
**Prerequisites:** None

## Course Objectives

After completion of the course, the student shall be able to:

1. Develop a deeper understanding of descriptive and numerical methods for describing data and numerical measures such as mean, median, mode, variance, range, standard deviation, and quartiles.
2. Develop an understanding of concepts and methods in probability theory.
3. Understand discrete and continuous probability distributions and their applications in problem solving.
4. Learn and apply tools for curve fitting via linear regression and correlation.

## Course Learning Outcomes

### CLO-01
Explain the knowledge of the basic theory, axioms and fundamental laws of probability.

### CLO-02
Use probabilistic and statistical methodology in computing applications.

### CLO-03
Analyze and distinguish different methods of probability in order to determine and estimate different probability models.
