# MA-213 — Discrete Mathematics

**University:** Sarhad University of Science & Information Technology, Peshawar  
**Department:** Computer Science and IT  
**Credit Hours:** 3-0  
**Course Duration:** One Semester  
**Credit Units:** 3  
**Level:** 2nd Semester, 1st Year  
**Prerequisites:** None

## Course Objectives

This course aims to develop the students’ ability of logical thinking and its application to computer science. Via teaching variety of important concepts, this course aims to develop students understanding of breadth of mathematics and familiarity with the concepts, structures and algorithms that are essential to the field of computer science and engineering.

## Course Learning Outcomes

### CLO-01
Recall the basic concepts of logic and proofs, set theory, relations in functions and complexity of algorithms.

### CLO-02
Demonstrate the fundamental concepts of counting, graphs, trees, and Mathematical Induction.

### CLO-03
Apply the knowledge learnt to solve mathematical problems in computer science and engineering disciplines.
