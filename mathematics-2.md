## Course Description

This course builds on the fundamental concepts of mathematics, exploring functions, limits, derivatives, and integration. It introduces differential equations, analytical geometry, linear inequalities, conic sections, and vectors. The course emphasizes both theoretical understanding and practical application of mathematical principles.

## Course Learning Outcomes

### CLO-01
Understand and apply the concepts of functions, limits, and continuity.

### CLO-02
Differentiate and integrate various functions, including polynomial, trigonometric, and exponential functions.

### CLO-03
Analyze and solve problems involving differential equations, analytical geometry, and vectors.

## Week-wise Breakdown

### Week 1
- Concept of function
- Domain and range of a function
- Notation and value of a function
- Types of functions
- Graph sketching

### Week 2
- Limits of a function
- Theorems on limits
- Left- and right-hand limits
- Limits at infinity

### Week 3
- Continuous and discontinuous functions
- Criteria for existence of a limit of function
- Continuity of a function at a point
- Continuity at entire domain

### Week 4
- Dependent and independent variables
- Average rate of change
- Derivative of a function
- Notation for derivative
- Derivative as limit of a function

### Week 5
- Theorems on derivative
- Chain Rule of derivative
- Derivative of inverse functions

### Week 6
- Derivative of polynomial functions
- Derivative of parametric functions
- Derivative of implicit relations

### Week 7
- Derivative of trigonometric and inverse trigonometric functions
- Derivative of exponential functions
- Logarithmic functions

### Week 8
- Derivative of hyperbolic functions and inverse hyperbolic functions
- Higher order derivatives

### Week 9
- Series expansions of functions
- Maclaurin theorem
- Taylor’s series expansion of functions
- Geometrical interpretation of derivative
- Increasing and decreasing functions
- Maxima and minima

### Week 10
- Integration as anti-derivative
- Theorems on anti-derivative
- Integration by substitution method
- By parts method of integration
- Integration of partial fractions

### Week 11
- Definition of Differential equation
- Order and degree of differential equation
- Solution of 1st order differential equation

### Week 12
- Introduction to analytical geometry
- Types of straight lines
- Translation and rotation of axes
- General equation of 2nd degree

### Week 13
- Line through the intersection of two lines
- Distance of point from a line
- Area of triangular region
- Angle between two lines

### Week 14
- Linear inequalities
- Graphing of linear inequalities in two variables
- Feasible solution set
- Linear programming
- Linear programming problems

### Week 15
- Introduction to conic sections
- Equation of a circle in different forms
- Tangent and normal
- Equation of parabola, ellipse, and hyperbola

### Week 16
- Introduction to scalars and vectors
- Algebra of vectors
- Properties of vectors
- Dot and cross product of vectors
