# GE 109 — Ideology & Constitution of Pakistan

**University:** Sarhad University of Science & Information Technology, Peshawar  
**Credit Hours:** 2-0  
**Course Duration:** One Semester  
**Credit Units:** 2  
**Level:** Undergraduate  
**Prerequisites:** None

## Course Description

This course provides an in-depth exploration of Pakistan's ideology historical background, reformative periods and political and constitutional evolution. It covers the ideological foundations of Pakistan, the reformative period in Mughal and British era, and the country's post-independence constitutions and political development. Students will gain insight into Pakistan's ideological foundation, visions of different reforms and political and constitutional time fostering a sense of patriotism and understanding of their responsibilities as citizens.

## Course Objectives

The course aims to:

- Enhance students' knowledge of Pakistan's ideological foundations, politics, and constitutional development.
- Foster a sense of patriotism and responsibility towards the nation.
- Equip students with an understanding of their role in the socio-political and constitutional context of Pakistan.

## Course Learning Outcomes

### CLO-01
Describe the ideological foundations of Pakistan and the historical movements leading to its establishment.

### CLO-02
Analyze the political and constitutional development of Pakistan, including key events and documents.

### CLO-03
Evaluate Pakistan's post-independence constitutions, political situation and amendments.
